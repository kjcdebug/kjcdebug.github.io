My Daily Brief

Hire yourself a chief of staff. It reads your email and calendar so your morning starts with a plan, not a pile. About 10 minutes to set up.

  1. Connect Outlook (one time): in ChatGPT, open Plugins, search Outlook, and add Outlook Email and Outlook Calendar (and Microsoft Teams if you use it). Sign in and click Accept.
  2. Copy the prompt with the button below.
  3. Paste it into a new chat in your company ChatGPT account and send it.
  4. Check the brief against your real inbox and calendar. Tell it what to fix ("shorter," "skip newsletters").
  5. Make it daily: in the same chat, send the line below.
Send this to schedule it:
Run this brief for me every weekday at 7:00 AM.
Company work goes in your company ChatGPT account. Never a personal one. It only reads; it never sends anything for you. You are responsible for checking what it tells you.
Act as my chief of staff. Use my Outlook email and calendar (and Teams, if it's connected) to brief me on my day.

Give me:
1. The one thing: the single most important thing about today, in one sentence.
2. Today: each meeting with the time, who is in it, and anything I should prepare or bring.
3. Top 5: the five things I should do today, most important first, one line each. Include decisions only I can make, emails from real people waiting on me (say how many days they've waited), and anything I said I would do that isn't done.
4. Waiting on others: anything I'm waiting on that is going stale, with how many days.
5. Heads up: meetings in the next 3 days I haven't prepared for, or anything else I'd otherwise miss. Three at most.

Rules:
- Keep it to one screen. Short lines, no long paragraphs.
- Say each item once. If it's in the top 5, don't repeat it anywhere else.
- Only use what you can actually see in my email, calendar and Teams. Never make anything up. If a section has nothing real, leave it out.
- Skip newsletters, automatic replies and system notifications.
- Label anything you had to guess as ASSUMPTION.
- Never send, reply to, forward, delete or accept anything. Just brief me.

End by asking me: "Anything you want me to change in this brief?"

Schedule not showing up?

Check that Outlook is connected under Plugins, then send the schedule line again. Still stuck? Bookmark this page and paste the prompt into a new chat in the morning while you sort it out.
